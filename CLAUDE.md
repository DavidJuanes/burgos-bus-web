# burgos-bus-web

Static site for the Burgos Bus Android app (`dev.davidjuanes.burgosbus`; app repo: GitLab `salamanca-bus/salamanca-bus`, flavor `burgos`, spec in its `docs/app-spec.md` §13.6). Jekyll 4, built by GitHub Actions, served by GitHub Pages at https://burgosbus.davidjuanes.dev/ behind Cloudflare. It started as a copy of `zamora-bus-web` (GitHub `DavidJuanes/zamora-bus-web`), itself a copy of `avila-bus-web` and of `salamanca-bus-web`: fixes to the shared layout, CSS or JS usually belong in both. Push straight to `main`; every push deploys.

- **i18n without plugins.** Spanish at `/`, English at `/en/`. Landing texts live in `_data/i18n/{es,en}.yml` and are rendered by `_includes/landing.html`; each page sets `lang` and a shared `ref` so the layout can find its translation (language switch, `hreflang`). Change both languages together. No automatic redirect by browser language.
- **Privacy policy** (`privacidad.md`, `en/privacy.md`) is the URL declared in Play Console. It must match the app's Data safety form (described in the app repo's `docs/play-listing.md`): when the app starts or stops collecting something, update both policies and their `updated` date.
- **`app-ads.txt`** holds the AdMob publisher line (`pub-0847996848911499`). AdMob crawls it from the developer website set in the Play listing.
- **Images** in `assets/img` come from the app repo's `burgos` flavor: phone screens recorded with Roborazzi like `BurgosScreenshotTest` (Spanish with `es`, English with `en-rGB`; `nearby-dark-clock` with the arrival format set to clock time), converted to WebP (600 × 1300) with ImageMagick. `screens/*/map.webp` comes from the emulator instead (600 × 1270, without the status bar), because Roborazzi can't draw the map; location simulated at the Cathedral, lines 1–4 shown. The icons and Open Graph images come from the app's launcher icon (ochre gradient); the Open Graph ones were rendered from HTML with headless Chromium. `gallery.shots` in `_data/i18n` is empty until Burgos has Play listing graphics (empty hides the gallery).
- **Colours** are Burgos's: the provisional ochre palette of the app's `src/burgos/.../ui/theme/Color.kt` (the web's `--brand` is the app's darker `BrandPrimaryDark` for contrast) and the line colours of `bus-data-burgos` (`_data/lines.yml`, unsoftened as in the app; text colour by contrast, as the app does).
- **`play_live`** in `_config.yml`: true shows the Google Play badge, linking to the listing; false would show «Próximamente en Google Play» in its place. It is true even though Burgos Bus is not on Play yet (decision of the user), so the badge links to a listing that does not exist yet.
- **No third parties at runtime:** fonts are self-hosted (latin subset, OFL), no analytics, no cookies. The privacy policy says so; keep it true.
- **Motion:** native CSS (scroll-driven animations) and vanilla JS in `assets/js/main.js`; everything stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The Google Play badge is Google's official image; don't edit it, and keep the trademark line in the footer.
